Tier 1 professionals—such as lawyers, accountants, and healthcare providers—cannot compromise on data privacy. A truly free, entirely local PDF merger is not just a convenience; it is a professional necessity.

2. How Client-Side Processing Works

The magic behind MergeLab lies in modern browser capabilities and the underlying JavaScript architecture. When you drag and drop a PDF file into the workspace, the file never travels across the internet. Instead, your browser reads the file's raw binary data (ArrayBuffer) directly from your local hard drive.

We utilize an advanced library called pdf-lib, which decodes the PDF structure, isolates individual pages, and allows us to rebuild a completely new document mathematically within your device's memory (RAM). When you select a page range or rotate a document, the math is recalculated locally. When you click merge, the final PDF is generated instantly, and your browser triggers a standard download. Zero APIs, zero wait times for uploading, and absolute zero risk of data interception.

Yes, this tool on Forgenos is 100% free forever. There are no paywalls, no signup prompts, and no hidden subscriptions.

Absolutely not. We use client-side processing, meaning the merging happens entirely inside your own web browser. Your files never touch our servers, ensuring total privacy.

Because there is no upload, we do not impose artificial limits. You are only limited by the RAM available on your device. Most modern computers handle 500MB+ merges easily.

Yes! The tool features a visual drag-and-drop interface. Just click and drag the handle on the left side of any file card to change its position in the final document.

Yes, MergeLab is fully responsive. You can upload, reorder, and merge PDF files seamlessly on iOS and Android devices directly in Safari or Chrome.
