Professional PDF Watermarking, Delivered Locally

WatermarkLab is designed for professionals who need to protect their documents without compromising privacy. Unlike server-based tools that upload your sensitive documents to the cloud, WatermarkLab processes everything entirely within your web browser. This means your financial reports, legal contracts, and confidential drafts remain strictly on your device.

Key Features

  • Live Canvas Preview: See exactly how your watermark will look on the actual document before downloading.
  • Zero Server Uploads: Maximum security through 100% client-side JavaScript processing.
  • Total Customization: Control font face, size, hex colors, opacity, and rotation angles.
  • Smart Placement: Choose diagonal classic styles or 9-point grid positioning.
  • Batch Processing: Apply to all pages, exclude covers, or select specific page ranges instantly.

How to Use

  1. Upload your PDF file using the drop zone above.
  2. Enter your desired text or select a quick preset like "CONFIDENTIAL".
  3. Use the sliders to adjust opacity and size while watching the live preview update.
  4. Choose which pages should receive the watermark.
  5. Click "Add Watermark" to generate and download your new file instantly.

Common Use Cases

  • • Legal Teams: Stamping "PRIVILEGED & CONFIDENTIAL" on discovery documents.
  • • Financial Planners: Adding "DRAFT" to unfinalized wealth projections.
  • • Creators & Authors: Protecting manuscripts with "© 2026 Copyright" marks.
  • • HR Departments: Marking "INTERNAL USE ONLY" on policy drafts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this PDF watermark tool completely free?

Yes, this tool on Forgenos is 100% free. No signup, no subscription, no hidden fees.

How do I add a DRAFT watermark to my PDF?

Simply upload your PDF, click the 'DRAFT' preset button, adjust the opacity and angle if needed using the live preview, and click Add Watermark to download your file.

Is my PDF file uploaded to a server?

No. WatermarkLab processes your PDF entirely within your browser using local JavaScript. Your file never leaves your device, ensuring complete privacy and security.

Can I choose which pages get watermarked?

Yes. Under the 'Apply To' section, you can select All Pages, specific page ranges (like 1-3, 5), or exclude the first/last pages.

Can I change the watermark font and color?

Absolutely. You can choose from standard fonts like Helvetica, Times Roman, and Courier, pick from 8 preset colors or a custom hex color, and adjust the opacity to your liking.
