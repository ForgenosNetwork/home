    How Our Title Case Generator Works

    Basic converters apply a naive approach to title casing: they simply capitalize the first letter of every single word. This violates standard style guides (like AP or Chicago Style). Our Smart Title Case engine analyzes the sentence structure and correctly leaves articles (a, an, the), coordinating conjunctions (and, but, or), and short prepositions (in, of, on) in lowercase, unless they are the first or last word of the title.

    Advanced Formats for Developers

    • kebab-case: Perfect for generating SEO-friendly URLs and CSS class names.
    • CONSTANT_CASE: Used for global constants and environment variables.
